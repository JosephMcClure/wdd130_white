Start Growing with Confidence

Want to bring a little green into your home but aren’t sure where to start? Growing Simple helps you choose a houseplant and understand its basic needs. You don’t need a large garden or expensive equipment to get started. A suitable plant, a pot with drainage holes, and a place with the right light are a good beginning.

Every home is different, so the best plant for you depends on the space you have. Before buying one, look at how much daylight reaches the spot where you want to put it. Then choose a plant that suits those conditions. Start with one plant so you can get used to checking its soil and watching how it grows.

Three Steps to Get Started

  1. Choose your spot. Find a place with natural light and enough room for the plant. Keep it away from heating vents and cold drafts.
  2. Pick a plant that fits. Read the Easy Plants page to compare pothos and snake plants.
  3. Learn its routine. Check the soil before watering and pay attention to changes in the leaves. Your plant’s needs can change with the season.

Small Habits Make a Difference

You don’t have to get everything right immediately. Take time to learn what your plant needs and make small adjustments. Our Care Guide explains watering, light, and drainage so you can build a simple care routine.
